# Refined prompt v1 — keep raw prompts private, publish only reviewed prompts

Raw prompt: `2026.11.19_raw_private_raw_prompts.md` (private after this change).
Status: **waiting for the owner's decisions (section 4). Nothing executed yet.**

## 1. Request

The owner's raw ("gross") prompts must not be published. Only the reviewed (refined) prompts are public. Two folders inside `prompts/`: one private for the owner, one published.

## 2. Today

- The repository is **public**. `prompts/` has 59 tracked files: 37 raw prompts and 22 reviewed files (refined plans and execution logs), in 8 topic folders, plus 2 untracked topic folders.
- 2 folders hold only raw prompts (`2026.11.18_whatsapp_status`, `2026.11.19_project_status`), and the untracked `2026.11.19_macos_keychain_prompts` too.
- Links to raw files: 8 refined plans (`Sources:` lines) and `docs/reference_mcp_analysis.md` (the owner's authorship confirmation). Plain mentions in backticks in several logs and plans.
- `CLAUDE.md` rule 6 says where raw prompts go; `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` link to the plan and the logs by path.

## 3. Plan (recommended options)

Names: **raw prompts** (the owner's words, verbatim) and **reviewed prompts** (refined plans, decisions, execution logs).

```
prompts/
  raw/            private: in .gitignore, only on the owner's computer
    2026.11.19_app_icon/2026.11.19_raw_app_icon.md
  reviewed/       published
    2026.11.19_app_icon/2026.11.19_refined_app_icon_v1.md
    2026.11.19_app_icon/2026.11.19_execution_log.md
  README.md       explains the two folders
```

Steps (branch `chore-private-raw-prompts`):
1. Move every `*_raw_*` file to `prompts/raw/<date_topic>/` and everything else to `prompts/reviewed/<date_topic>/` (git keeps the history of moved reviewed files).
2. Add `prompts/raw/` to `.gitignore` and `git rm --cached` the raw files: they stay on disk, but leave the repository from the next commit.
3. Links: in reviewed files, a link to a raw prompt becomes plain text ("raw prompt, private"); paths in `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, the docs, and the logs point to `prompts/reviewed/...`. The docs link check proves nothing is broken.
4. `docs/reference_mcp_analysis.md`: the authorship line keeps its words and date, without the link.
5. `CLAUDE.md` rule 6, new text: "Save every owner prompt verbatim in `prompts/raw/<YYYY.MM.DD>_<topic>/<YYYY.MM.DD>_raw_<topic>.md` (private, ignored by git), and save refinements, decisions, and execution logs in `prompts/reviewed/<YYYY.MM.DD>_<topic>/` (published). Reviewed files never quote a raw prompt in full and never link to one."
6. `prompts/README.md` explains the two folders.
7. Pull request, green CI, merge.
