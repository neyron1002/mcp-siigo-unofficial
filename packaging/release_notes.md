**MCP Siigo Unofficial** connects AI assistants (Claude, Codex, Copilot, Gemini) to your Siigo accounting data through a local MCP server. Free, MIT License, not affiliated with Siigo S.A.S.

### Download

| System | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64) | [MCPSiigoUnofficial-{version}-windows-x64.exe](https://github.com/neyron1002/mcp-siigo-unofficial/releases/download/{tag}/MCPSiigoUnofficial-{version}-windows-x64.exe) |
| macOS 13+ (Apple silicon) | [MCPSiigoUnofficial-{version}-macos-arm64.dmg](https://github.com/neyron1002/mcp-siigo-unofficial/releases/download/{tag}/MCPSiigoUnofficial-{version}-macos-arm64.dmg) |
| Ubuntu 22.04/24.04 (x64) | [mcp-siigo-unofficial-{version}-linux-x64](https://github.com/neyron1002/mcp-siigo-unofficial/releases/download/{tag}/mcp-siigo-unofficial-{version}-linux-x64) |

The files without a version in the name (for example `MCPSiigoUnofficial-windows-x64.exe`) are the same files, for links that always point to the newest version.

Start with the [install guide](https://github.com/neyron1002/mcp-siigo-unofficial/blob/{tag}/docs/install.md), then click **Connect** in the app to connect your AI apps (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Gemini CLI, VS Code) in one step: see [client setup](https://github.com/neyron1002/mcp-siigo-unofficial/blob/{tag}/docs/clients.md). `SHA256SUMS` is signed with Ed25519 (`SHA256SUMS.sig`); the app's updater checks it automatically.

Write actions create **real** records in Siigo. They are off by default, turn off at every restart, and every write needs your approval. You are responsible for the actions your AI client executes.
